The Skatemill

Train in place.
Skate like it counts.

The Prowins Skatemill brings roller skating practice indoors, so skaters can work on form and stamina without needing a rink.

How it works

Step on, strap in and skate. The Skatemill keeps you moving in place so every stride can be coached and repeated.

Why skaters love it

Practise technique and build endurance in one spot, with the same feel as the rink and none of the waiting for space.

At the Nationals

Find the Skatemill on the floor at the Skating Nationals and try it yourself during the event.
